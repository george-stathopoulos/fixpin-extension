# Changelog

Changes to the FixPin browser extension, newest first. The whole project's changelog (app and website too) is at https://george-stathopoulos.github.io/fixpin/changelog/

## 0.1.2 (2026-10-19)
- The box is now drawn while you drag, so you can see what you are selecting.
- Clicking or typing in the note panel no longer adds a pin. Picking has its own layer under the panel.
- The panel moves to the side of the page that has no marked spots.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-19)
- Renamed to FixPin (from the working name FixSpot).

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-18)
- First release for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox: capture the page, the marked spots, and page errors from the moment you click.
